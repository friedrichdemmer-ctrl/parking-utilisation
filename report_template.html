    How this was measured

    • Occupancy is 1 − free ÷ capacity. Every 15 minutes the latest reading is taken and carried forward for up to 12 hours, because many feeds only report when the count changes. Samples are averaged into the 168 hours of the week, so every hour counts equally however often a feed reports.
    • Times are each garage's local time. Country and city figures weight each garage by its capacity, so they are the share of all spaces occupied.
    • Counts below zero or above capacity (counters drifting) are treated as full or empty.

    Left out

      Read with care

      • The window runs from mid-August, so it includes the end of the summer holidays in most countries. Expect weekday figures to rise in a term-time sample.
      • Coverage differs by city. Several feeds were added in late September and contribute one or two weeks rather than eight.
      • The UK sample is mostly park-and-ride sites at metro stations, which run far emptier than city-centre garages. Park-and-ride sites overall average about a quarter occupied.
