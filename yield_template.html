    What that is worth

    Garages grouped by hourly rate: how full they run, and what a space earns.

    By city

    Cities with eight or more priced garages. Rates converted to euros for comparison.

    By kind of car park

    Busy and cheap

    Garages of 100 spaces or more running fuller than the median on a lower-than-median rate. On the evidence above, the likeliest places to raise price without losing custom.

    Quiet and dear

    The reverse: emptier than the median on a higher rate. Price may not be the cause — these are often garages in the wrong place, or with a cheaper one next door.

    How this was measured

      What is left out

        Read with care

        • Takings are modelled, not reported: measured occupancy, published tariffs, and an assumed mix of stay lengths. They are right in aggregate and roughest for any single garage.
        • A correlation is not a licence to raise prices. Dearer garages sit in busier places, which is most of why they stay full; the figures here cannot separate the two.
